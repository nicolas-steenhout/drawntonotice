I was relaxing in the living room when I picked up my sketchbook and drew this little coffee station. It is probably more “from life” than urban sketching.

![Ink and watercolor sketch of a kitchen counter with a stack of colorful cups, a coffee maker, a container, and a white temperature-controlled sourdough starter box beneath wooden cabinets.](/images/2026-04-11-coffee-station.jpg)

The device on the right is my sourdough home, which keeps my starter at 22°C. Not a great sketch, perhaps, but it was fun to draw.
