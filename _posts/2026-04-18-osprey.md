I went to check out where ospreys had nested in the past and found them again. I had my camera and took a few photos, but I spent more time sketching than anything else.

![Tall ink and watercolor sketch of two ospreys at a nest on a tall pole, with a second utility pole, a road, trees, and a parked car below; a handwritten note appears in the sky.](/images/2026-04-18-osprey.jpg)

It was one of the most relaxing times I’ve had in a long time.
