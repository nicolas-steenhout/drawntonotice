I went to workshops at the Urban Sketchers Vancouver workshop weekend on Granville Island. I did the first sketch during one of the workshops, but I wasn’t happy with it. I came back the following day and sketched the bridge again. I’m much happier with the second attempt.

![Ink and watercolor sketch of Burrard Bridge from the water, with its stone tower, steel truss span, boats, shoreline, and handwritten notes about the Granville Island Urban Sketchers workshop weekend. The page is dated May 31, 2026.](/images/2026-05-31-burrard-bridge.jpg)

Lots of passersby commented on the sketch as I worked. They were all very impressed.

The first sketch, made during Rita Sabler’s workshop, shows Granville Island and Burrard Bridge from across the water. Pretty much the same view from the same vantage point.

![Ink and watercolor sketch of Granville Island and Burrard Bridge, with the bridge spanning the upper half of an open sketchbook above a marina, boats, trees, and distant buildings. The page is dated May 30, 2026, and includes the Urban Sketchers Vancouver logo.](/images/2026-05-30-granville-workshop.jpg)
